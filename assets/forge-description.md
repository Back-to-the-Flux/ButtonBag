**Too many buttons around your minimap? Put them in a bag.**

ButtonBag gathers every addon's minimap button into one bag button. Click the bag and a panel drops
down holding all of them; click a button inside it as usual.

### Features

- **One button instead of many**: addon buttons move into the bag; the game's own minimap buttons
  stay put.
- **Works with any addon**: buttons are found automatically, including ones made a little late.
- **Choose what goes in**: untick any button in the settings to keep it on the minimap.
- **Your order**: A to Z, or arrange them yourself with Up and Down.
- **Open your way**: on a click or on hover, toward the middle of the screen or always down, up,
  left or right.
- **Size and shape**: Small to Huge buttons, 1 to 6 per row.
- **Folds itself up** after a click, when the mouse moves away (0.5 to 5 seconds) or on Escape.
- **Nothing is lost**: turn collecting off and every button goes back exactly where it was.
- Works with square minimaps, and the settings window matches the game's own menus.

### Use

Left-click the bag to open it, right-click for settings, drag to move it.
`/bbag` settings · `/bbag open` open the bag · `/bbag scan` look for buttons again
